Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

A focused pixel editor built for people who ship. Fast, keyboard-first, and made for sprites.

✎

Full toolset

Pencil, eraser, bucket fill, eyedropper, line, rectangle, and ellipse. Every tool you actually use.

▤

Unlimited layers

Stack, rename, hide, and reorder layers. Each layer is fully independent with its own opacity.

↶

Real undo history

Full state snapshots on every action. Undo and redo up to 50 steps back. Nothing is ever lost.

⌘

Keyboard-first

Every tool and action has a shortcut. Draw at the speed of thought without touching a menu.

⬇

Clean exports

Export at 1×, 4×, 8×, or 16× scale. PNG output with a transparent background, crisp pixels, no artifacts.

⚡

Instant autosave

Every stroke saves to your browser. Close the tab, come back tomorrow, and everything is right where you left it.
